# V28 — Episodes & Seasons on the watch screen
- TV watch screens now include an episode browser beneath the player, with season selection, episode stills, titles, runtimes and descriptions from TMDB.
- The currently selected episode is highlighted; a Next Episode button appears when the next episode is available.
- Selecting an episode navigates to the same watch page with updated season and episode parameters. The existing player/source implementation is unchanged.
- Movie watch pages are unchanged. Episode metadata requires a working TMDB_TOKEN.
