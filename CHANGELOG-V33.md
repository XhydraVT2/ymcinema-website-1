# V33 — Songs & Music (preview)
- Adds a Songs & Music section after Trailer on movie pages only.
- Lists TMDB-attached YouTube videos labelled as music/song, excluding trailers.
- Optional YOUTUBE_API_KEY (YouTube Data API v3) discovers official song videos with title matching and channel filtering.
- Click a thumbnail to load a privacy-enhanced YouTube iframe; no autoplay.
- Shows a YouTube search link if no verified songs are found.
- To revert this experiment, use V32 or ask to remove the section.
